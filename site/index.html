Known issue: some Premium accounts can’t play music

Some Spotify Premium accounts get Audio key refused (0x0001) on every song. This is a Spotify problem, not a 3DSpotify bug: Spotify’s servers refuse the audio key for those accounts, and other unofficial clients hit the same error (librespot #1649). There is no fix yet, but we are actively working on solving it, or at least on finding an alternative.

Features

Straight to Spotify

The console connects to Spotify over HTTPS by itself. No PC, proxy or phone app in between.

Search songs

Type with the system keyboard and browse results four at a time, with cover thumbnails. The search runs in the background.

Plays on the console

Audio is decoded and played locally, with the rest of the results as the queue, the album art and its own volume.

Music never stops

Four tabs (Home, Search, Playing and Settings) and the track keeps playing whichever one you open.

Sign in without typing passwords

Link your account with a short code on spotify.com/pair or by scanning a QR code with your phone.

English and Spanish

Switch the language at any moment with SELECT, on any screen.

You will need

A Spotify Premium account and a Nintendo 3DS with Wi-Fi and the Homebrew Launcher. Installing the .cia also requires custom firmware.

Controls

Button What it does
A Confirm: open the keyboard, play the highlighted song, pause or resume.
B Go back, or cancel a pending search or pairing.
Y New search from the results; pair again after a sign-in error.
D-Pad Move the focus. In results, left/right jump four songs; in the player, left/right change track and up/down set the volume.
LR Previous or next tab (ZL/ZR too on New 3DS).
SELECT Switch between English and Spanish.
START Exit the app.
Touch Tap tabs, buttons and songs; the action runs when you lift the stylus inside. Drag lists to scroll.

Installation

Pick one of the two formats from the latest release.

.3dsx

Homebrew Launcher
  1. Download 3DSpotify.3dsx.
  2. Copy it to SD:/3ds/3DSpotify/.
  3. Open it from the Homebrew Launcher.
Download .3dsx

.cia

FBI · HOME Menu
  1. Scan the QR code from FBI, or download 3DSpotify.cia and copy it to the SD card.
  2. Install it with FBI (requires custom firmware).
  3. Open it from the HOME Menu. Settings are kept in SD:/3ds/spotibacan/.
Download .cia

First launch

The console shows a code on the top screen. Open spotify.com/pair on your phone or computer, sign in, enter the code and accept, or scan the QR code on the bottom screen.

The credential is saved to the SD card, so later launches connect on their own.
